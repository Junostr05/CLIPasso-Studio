# Benchmark photos

NASA's media guidelines ask that they are not used to imply an endorsement by NASA or by the people shown. They are scaled to at most 640 px for the quality benchmark (`tools/benchmark.py --suite`); `sources.json` holds the same list for the tool (with the label the judge checks).
